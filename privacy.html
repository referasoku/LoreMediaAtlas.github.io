Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Welcome to LoreMediaAtlas ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

When you register an account or interact with our app, we may collect:

  • Account Information: Your email address and username provided during signup.
  • User-Generated Content: Reviews, ratings, and comments you submit for movies and games.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Maintain and secure your user session and authentication state.
  • Display your reviews and username next to the media items you review.
  • Improve the functionality and user experience of LoreMediaAtlas.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is securely stored using Supabase, which handles database management and authentication. We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

4. Third-Party Services

Our app fetches media information from external APIs, including The Movie Database (TMDB) and the Internet Game Database (IGDB). These services have their own independent privacy policies.
